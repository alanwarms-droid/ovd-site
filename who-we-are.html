The firm

Operating experience, applied to a technology question.

OVD Advisory works with private equity firms and founder-owned businesses to establish which AI initiatives are worth funding, what each returns, and in what order the work should be done — and then to build them.

The firm was founded on a straightforward observation. Familiarity with what these tools can do is now widely held; judgment about which of them will pay inside a particular business is not. That judgment comes from having run companies — from knowing what a process looks like when it is sound, what an initiative actually costs once integration and adoption are counted, and which claims survive contact with a working operation.

Its principals have founded, scaled and exited businesses across technology, digital commerce, consumer products, hospitality, gaming, food and beverage, and manufacturing, and have served on the boards of a dozen more. They also invest, through OVD Capital, which is disclosed at the outset of every engagement and means a business is examined the way an owner examines one.

The firm is based in Chicago and works nationally.

The partners

Alan has played key roles in acquiring, investing in and founding more than ten businesses over the last twenty years, running Participate Capital from 2005 to 2025 and launching OVD Capital in January 2026. Recent investments include Alliance Advisors and Hydro Flask, exited to Helen of Troy.

Gary Leff

Principal LinkedIn →

Gary has founded, scaled and invested in consumer, hospitality, gaming and food and beverage businesses for more than thirty years, with multiple successful exits. He has played lead roles building high-performing platforms in regulated gaming and experiential retail, including the creation and rapid growth of Laredo Hospitality Ventures, which scaled to 57 distributed gaming locations in under five years before being acquired by the largest distributed gaming company in the country at the time.

Earlier he founded Stir Crazy, a private-equity-backed Asian casual dining concept that grew to twelve locations across major U.S. markets and was acquired in 2006. He also co-founded and led the development and operations of Hatch, a leading cannabis dispensary company in the Illinois market.

Gary has been an investor in and advisor to Rivers Casino since 2009, contributing to the strategy and branding of the guest experience at one of Illinois' top-performing casinos, and has advised on several other projects for Rush Street Gaming. He was an investor and active board member of More Than Gourmet, a leading manufacturer of made-from-scratch classic French stocks and sauces, working closely with management to more than double sales and increase profitability, leading to a successful exit via a management-led recapitalization in 2016.

Gary began his career in strategy consulting at Bain & Company and Monitor Group. MBA, J.L. Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University; B.S. in Economics, The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania. He lives in Chicago.

On engagements: service and multi-unit operations — businesses in which knowledge resides with senior people and capacity constrains growth.
