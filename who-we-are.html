The firm

An advisory firm built out of operating experience.

OVD Advisory works with private equity firms and founder-owned businesses to identify the AI initiatives worth funding, quantify what each returns, and build them.

Knowing what these tools can do is now common. Knowing which of them will pay inside a particular business is not. That judgment comes from having run companies: from knowing what a sound process looks like, what an initiative costs once integration and adoption are counted, and which claims survive contact with a working operation.

The principals have founded, scaled and exited businesses across technology, digital commerce, consumer products, hospitality, gaming, food and beverage, and manufacturing, and served on the boards of a dozen more. Each began at Bain, BCG or Monitor. Three decades operating since.

The firm is based in Chicago and works nationally.

Capabilities

Data and agent systems. Fleets of agents constructed against internal and public data to size markets, generate qualified leads and forecast revenue.

Retrieval over a company's own corpus. Making accumulated documents, history and institutional knowledge searchable and usable, privately.

Workflow and coordination. Structured capture of work that currently goes unrecorded, and automation of the coordination that consumes operating capacity.

Reporting and forecasting. Replacing hand-built management reporting with pipelines that produce it, and forecasts built from the bottom up.

Performance marketing. Where machine-generated creative works, and where it does not.

The investing arm

The principals invest their own capital through OVD Capital, an operator-led private equity firm acquiring profitable founder-owned businesses. It is a separate firm, disclosed at the outset of every engagement and never its purpose. It is also why a business is examined the way an owner examines one.

Gary Leff

Principal LinkedIn →

Gary has founded, scaled and invested in consumer, hospitality, gaming and food and beverage businesses for more than thirty years, with multiple successful exits. He has played lead roles building high-performing platforms in regulated gaming and experiential retail, including the creation and rapid growth of Laredo Hospitality Ventures, which scaled to 57 distributed gaming locations in under five years before being acquired by the largest distributed gaming company in the country at the time.

Gary has been an investor in and advisor to Rivers Casino since 2009, contributing to the strategy and branding of the guest experience at one of Illinois' top-performing casinos, and has advised on several other projects for Rush Street Gaming. He was an investor and active board member of More Than Gourmet, a leading manufacturer of made-from-scratch classic French stocks and sauces, working closely with management to more than double sales and increase profitability, leading to a successful exit via a management-led recapitalization in 2016.

Gary began his career in strategy consulting at Bain & Company and Monitor Group. MBA, J.L. Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University; B.S. in Economics, The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania. He lives in Chicago.
