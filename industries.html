Industries

Where this work returns fastest.

AI pays best in businesses whose cost structure is people doing knowledge work that repeats. The principals have operated across each of the sectors below.

01

Professional and business services

Firms where expertise is delivered by people and capacity sets the ceiling on growth. Knowledge held by senior staff, work that repeats client to client, and coordination consuming the day.

02

Compliance and regulated services

Businesses whose product is getting something exactly right, on a deadline, under rules that do not move. Document-heavy, deadline-driven, and unusually well suited to review and drafting support.

03

Software and data

Established products with real customers, where the roadmap has outrun the infrastructure around it and support, onboarding and reporting absorb engineering time.

04

Healthcare services

Administrative load, documentation, prior authorization and revenue cycle, where accuracy is not optional and a person stays in the loop by design.

05

Distribution and light manufacturing

Quoting, procurement, demand forecasting and supplier management, where margin leaks in a hundred small places rather than one large one.

06

Consumer and multi-unit operations

Businesses run across many sites, where consistency is the discipline and the same coordination happens in every location every week.

What the sectors have in common

Substantial hours going into work that repeats. Knowledge held by a few senior people rather than in any system. The same information re-entered across three or four applications. A process nobody has had time to fix because there has always been something more urgent.

Where a business has that shape, the work returns. Where it does not, we say so on the first call.
