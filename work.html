Recent engagements
Luxury travel · ~$60M volume

An AI opportunity assessment for a founder-owned travel firm

A twenty-plus advisor firm with an ambition to double in five years, constrained not by demand but by throughput — it could effectively onboard about one new advisor a year, because the training capacity sat with a handful of senior people. Thirty years of itineraries, trip reports, and client feedback existed but could not be searched.

Over three weeks we interviewed leadership and advisors, walked the systems in daily use, and read the firm's own SOP library, tool map, and wish list. We returned twelve opportunities in four themes, each scored and tiered, with a ninety-day path and a governance model.

The recommendation that mattered: do not replace the software. A platform migration would consume a year of attention and retrain twenty people before anything improved. An AI layer over the systems already in place pays back in weeks — and the durable advantage is the firm's own corpus, which no vendor can replicate.

A redacted version of the full assessment is available on request, pending the client's approval to publish.

Consumer products · Performance marketing

Automating the ad cycle, end to end — and finding its limits

We set out to automate an entire loop on Meta: ideation, creative generation, launch, and test readout, with as little human intervention as we could get away with. Not a pilot of one step — the whole cycle.

Parts of it worked better than we expected. One part that the market broadly treats as solved turned out to be the binding constraint, and no amount of additional automation moved it.

We are writing this one up properly, including the parts that did not work, because that is the half nobody publishes.
