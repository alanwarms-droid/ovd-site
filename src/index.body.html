Return, cost, capacity

  • Dollar return What it is worth in the accounts, discounted to today. Hours saved convert to dollars or they are not counted.
  • Cost and effort The whole cost. Integration nobody scoped. The exceptions that turn out to be half the volume. Training. The stretch before anything runs properly.
  • Organizational capacity What the company can absorb at once. Three initiatives run well finish ahead of nine run badly.

The same three tests in every business. For a fund, that means initiatives rank across the whole portfolio, not just within one company.

What operating experience changes

Knowing what these tools can do is now common. Knowing which of them will pay inside a particular business is not.

It shows up in four places. What survives contact with a working operation, as against a demonstration. What an initiative really costs once integration, exceptions and adoption are counted. Whether the underlying process is sound enough to build on. And which claims about this technology hold up.
