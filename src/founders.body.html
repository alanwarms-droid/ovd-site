For founder-owned businesses

Twenty years of work should not be exposed to an experiment.

Most owners of good businesses have reached the same position: the technology plainly matters, there is no internal team capable of separating the useful from the noise, and nobody is prepared to bet the company on finding out. That is the gap OVD Advisory works in.

The engagement

Three weeks, and a document that can be acted on without the firm.

Every relationship begins the same way: two to three weeks inside the business, concluding in a ranked schedule of where AI creates value here, what each opportunity is worth, what it costs, and the order in which the work should be done.

The document belongs to the owner. It can be run with an internal team, handed to an existing partner, or set aside. No obligation attaches to it and there is no automatic next step.

Where a company has no one with the hours to build the work — which is the usual case in a business of this size — OVD Advisory will run it, until each initiative is live and a named person inside has taken it over.

Fit

Where this tends to work.

The businesses in which AI repays fastest share a shape. People spend substantial hours on work that repeats; knowledge resides with a few senior people rather than in any system; and the same information is re-entered across three or four applications. Services firms, distribution, light manufacturing, healthcare, financial services and multi-unit operations.

Profitable, founder-owned, generally $1MM or more in EBITDA, with real customers and proven unit economics — businesses that are sound rather than broken, and constrained by infrastructure rather than by demand.

Where that is not the case, the firm will say so on the first call rather than after an engagement.

Disclosed

The principals also invest.

Alan Warms and Gary Leff invest their own capital, alongside Jamie Crouthamel, through OVD Capital. It is a separate firm and a separate conversation, and it is never the stated purpose of an engagement.

It is worth stating plainly at the outset because it shapes the advisory work rather than compromising it. A business is examined the way an owner would examine it, because the people examining it do that for a living. Occasionally, where both parties come to agree on what a business could be worth and both want to pursue it together, the conversation goes further.

Where it does, the founder retains a meaningful stake in the value newly identified — which is the value the preceding three weeks were spent establishing.
