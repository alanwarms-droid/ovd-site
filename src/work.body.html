Recent engagements
Luxury travel · ~$60M volume

An AI Opportunity Assessment for a founder-owned travel firm

A twenty-plus advisor firm with an ambition to double in five years, constrained not by demand but by throughput — it could effectively onboard about one new advisor a year, because the training capacity sat with a handful of senior people. Thirty years of itineraries, trip reports, and client feedback existed but could not be searched.

Over three weeks the principals interviewed leadership and advisors, reviewed the systems in daily use, and read the firm's own procedure library, tool map and internal wish list. The engagement returned twelve opportunities across four themes, each scored and tiered, with a ninety-day plan and a governance model.

The consequential recommendation was not to replace the software. A platform migration would have consumed a year of attention and retrained twenty people before anything improved. An AI layer over the systems already in place returns in weeks, and the durable advantage lies in the firm's own corpus, which no vendor can replicate.

A redacted version of the full assessment is available on request, subject to the client's approval to publish.

Consumer products · Performance marketing

Automating the ad cycle end to end, and finding its limits

The object was to automate an entire loop on Meta — ideation, creative generation, launch and test readout — with as little human intervention as the process would tolerate. Not a pilot of a single step, but the whole cycle.

Several stages performed better than anticipated. One step the market broadly treats as settled proved to be the binding constraint, and no amount of further automation moved it.

The work is being written up in full, including what did not succeed, since that is the half rarely published.
