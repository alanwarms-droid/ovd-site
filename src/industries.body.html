Industries

Two kinds of business, both of which we have run.

The principals operated in each of these sectors before advising anyone in them.

Knowledge and services

Software and data. Compliance and regulated services. Professional and business services. Healthcare services.

Expertise is the product and people deliver it. Capacity is the constraint on growth. Work recurs at volume, and the day goes to coordination, review, and getting things exactly right on a deadline.

Consumer and physical goods

Food and beverage. Branded consumer and outdoor products. Consumer manufacturing. Distribution. Specialty retail and direct-to-consumer.

The discipline here is consistency across many sites, a supply chain that holds margin, and demand you can forecast.
