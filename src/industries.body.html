Industries

Two kinds of business, both of which we have run.

The principals operated across each of these sectors before advising anyone in them.

Knowledge and services

Software and data, compliance and regulated services, professional and business services, and healthcare services. Businesses where expertise is the product and people deliver it — where capacity is the constraint on growth, work recurs at volume, and the day is consumed by coordination, review and getting things exactly right on a deadline.

Consumer and physical goods

Food and beverage, branded consumer and outdoor products, consumer manufacturing, distribution, and specialty retail and direct-to-consumer. Businesses where the discipline is consistency across many sites, a supply chain that does not leak margin, and demand you can actually forecast.
