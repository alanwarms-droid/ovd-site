What we do

The question isn't what AI can do. It's where, in this business, repetition is costing you money.

Most AI advice arrives as a maturity model and a twelve-month roadmap, written by people who have never carried a P&L. It surveys the technology. It doesn't tell an owner which two things to fund.

We start from the other end. We interview the people doing the work, map the processes they actually run, and come back with a ranked list of opportunities — each one scored on value, cost, complexity, risk, and time to impact. Then we tell you which ones we'd fund if the business were ours.

No platform to buy. No transformation program. Usually no new software at all — migration is the most expensive thing a company your size can purchase, and an AI layer on the systems you already run pays back in weeks instead of years.

How engagements run

Start small. Nobody signs a transformation on day one.

  1. 01 AI Opportunity Assessment Interviews, process mapping, a ranked opportunity matrix, and a ninety-day path. Fixed scope, fixed fee. 2–3 weeks
  2. 02 Program driver One outside owner of execution — runs the pilots, builds or configures the tools, manages the vendors, reports on a fixed cadence. Part-time and consistent beats full-time and episodic. Monthly
  3. 03 Implementation Build the two or three things that matter, with the people to do it. Documented handoff, no dependency on us. Per initiative
  4. 04 Capital Where the fit is right and both sides want it, we invest our own money alongside partners. Deal by deal — never the stated purpose of an engagement. If it fits
Who we work with

Two kinds of client, one question.

Private Equity

Every portco is doing something with AI. Very few are doing anything that reaches the P&L.

  • Find what's real — the two or three things in a portfolio company that actually pay, and the ones that will consume a year and deliver a demo.
  • Compare across the book — the same method and scoring in every company, so you can rank opportunities across the portfolio rather than within one.
  • Then drive it — a program driver who owns execution, or a clean handover to your own team.
